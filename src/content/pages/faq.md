## When does the Ball drop?

At 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on December 31, the Ball begins its descent as millions of voices count down the final seconds of the year. The lighted Ball descends 90 feet in sixty seconds, reaching the bottom at midnight, when the numerals of the new year light up above Times Square.

## Where is the Ball?

Atop One Times Square, the building at the intersection of 42nd Street, Seventh Avenue and Broadway. The Ball rises and descends on a 139-foot mast on the building’s roof.

## Can I watch the Ball Drop online?

Yes. The live, commercial-free [NYE webcast](/webcast) starts at 6:00 p.m. ET on December 31 with the lighting and raising of the Ball, and runs through midnight. You can also watch the Ball live any time of year on the five [Ball cams](/ball-cam-1).

## How big is the Ball?

The Constellation Ball is 12.5 feet in diameter, weighs 12,350 pounds, and is covered in 5,280 circular Waterford crystals, each lit by an LED light puck. See all the [Ball facts](/ball-facts).

## How many different Balls have there been?

Nine versions of the Ball have been designed since the first one dropped in 1907, from a 700-pound iron and wood Ball lit by one hundred light bulbs to today’s Constellation Ball. Read the [Ball history](/ball-history).

## Has the Ball dropped every year?

Every year since 1907, except 1942 and 1943, when the ceremony was suspended because of the wartime “dimout” of lights in New York City. Crowds still gathered in Times Square in those years and greeted the New Year with a minute of silence followed by the ringing of chimes.

## When did New Year’s Eve in Times Square begin?

In 1904, when The New York Times celebrated the opening of its new headquarters, the Times Tower, with an all-day street festival and fireworks. The first Ball was lowered from the tower’s flagpole three years later, to mark the arrival of 1908. Read the [NYE history](/nye-history).

## How much confetti is released at midnight?

3,000 pounds, released from the rooftops of buildings throughout Times Square. It includes tens of thousands of wishes written by people from around the world.

## How can I add my wish to the confetti?

Each December, the Confetti Wishing Wall opens in Times Square, and wishes can also be submitted online. Every wish is printed on a piece of confetti and released at midnight. Dates and details are announced each year in the [press releases](/press/releases).

## Can I visit the Ball up close?

Yes. One Times Square offers ticketed experiences, including the New Year’s Eve Ball Experience, the Skywalk Viewing Deck and the One Times Square Candy Experience. The Ball facts page [links to all of them](/ball-facts).

## Where can I find information about attending in person?

The Times Square Alliance, co-organizer of Times Square New Year’s Eve, publishes information for people attending the celebration at [timessquarenyc.org](https://www.timessquarenyc.org/).

## I’m a journalist. Where can I find photos and video?

The [Press](/press) section has hi-res photos, HD b-roll, pre-packaged video segments and press releases, all free to download for press use. Details of the live broadcast feed are in the [live TV feed media alert](/press/releases/2025-12-28-live-tv-feed-and-b-roll).
