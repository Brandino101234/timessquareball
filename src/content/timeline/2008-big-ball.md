The beauty and energy efficiency of the Centennial Ball inspired the building owners of One Times Square to build the permanent Big Ball 12 feet in diameter and weighing nearly six tons. The 2,688 crystal triangles were illuminated by 32,256 LEDs. This Big Times Square New Year’s Eve Ball was now a year-round attraction sparkling above Times Square in full public view from December 2008 through December 2024.
