In November of 2025, the ninth version of the Times Square New Year’s Eve Ball was revealed atop One Times Square. The new Constellation Ball is even bigger than the previous Big Ball. The Constellation Ball is 12.5 feet in diameter and weighs 12,350 pounds consisting of 5,280 crystal circles illuminated by 5,280 LED light pucks. The circular shapes evoke celestial bodies, perfectly embodying the new Ball’s Constellation theme.
